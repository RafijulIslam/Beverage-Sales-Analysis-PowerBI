# 🥤 Beverage Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI Beverage Sales Performance Dashboard** designed to analyze beverage sales, revenue, and profitability for 2022.

The dashboard provides insights into monthly and daily sales trends, beverage brand performance, and overall business results. It also includes interactive filters that allow users to explore sales performance across different divisions, districts, and beverage brands.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are to:

- Monitor overall beverage sales performance
- Analyze total revenue and profit
- Compare revenue contributions across beverage brands
- Identify monthly revenue trends and fluctuations
- Explore daily revenue patterns
- Compare revenue and profit across different beverage brands
- Enable interactive analysis through dashboard slicers
- Support data-driven business decision-making

## 🔍 Dashboard Features

### Interactive Slicers

Users can filter the dashboard by:

- Division
- District
- Beverage Brand

These filters enable users to explore sales performance across different geographical areas and beverage brands.

### Total Revenue by Month

The monthly revenue analysis tracks sales performance throughout 2022 and highlights changes in revenue over time.

- **Highest monthly revenue:** December, approximately 0.98M
- **Lowest monthly revenue:** March, approximately 0.46M

### Total Revenue by Beverage Brand

The donut chart compares the revenue contribution of different beverage brands, including:

- Coca-Cola
- Dasani Water
- Diet Coke
- Sprite
- Powerade
- Fanta

Coca-Cola represents the largest share of total revenue in the displayed dashboard, contributing approximately 23%.

### Total Revenue by Day

The daily revenue chart visualizes fluctuations in revenue across the days of the month, helping users explore sales patterns and identify periods of stronger or weaker performance.

### Revenue vs. Profit by Beverage Brand

The horizontal bar chart compares total revenue and total profit across beverage brands, making it easier to evaluate their relative financial contributions.
